How we work

From a source document to the Training Hub

You bring the source and the business decisions. Claude drafts the guide in the master template, keeps the validation register and this board up to date, and merges its pull requests. SMEs confirm the process facts, the approving SME and BPO sign off, and you publish. Four milestones stay separate, each with its own evidence:

Draft complete≠Questions closed≠Approved≠Published

The lanes

A card sits in one lane at a time, and the lane says whose turn it is. A card goes back and forth between Waiting for SME and Updating draft once for each question round. Blocked and Withdrawn guides open from the pills above the board.

  1. 1 · Intake

    Your turn. The ADO ticket is raised and the source is on its way: an SME draft, a legacy guide, a recording or meeting notes.

    Moves on when the source is in hand, the destination is agreed (one task is one topic in one guide, D-045) and the code is checked in the numbering register, or an O-number is given for a document that is not a procedure guide (D-070, D-076).

  2. 2 · Drafting

    Claude's turn. A Save As copy of the master template, completed to the authoring guide. Every gap gets a stable ID in the validation register. Notes to the author use ACTION: REVIEW REQUIREMENT (D-060).

    Moves on when the Word guide, Markdown preview and register are committed and the first question round is on the card.

  3. 3 · Waiting for SME

    The SME's turn. The card's questions are in the ADO ticket, or a review pack is with the SME.

    Moves on when the answers or the SME's Word comments come back.

  4. 4 · Updating draft

    Claude's turn. The answers go into the Word guide and the register. Items they close are closed; anything still open goes into the next round.

    Moves on when no blocking item is open, or the only open items are for the approver.

  5. 5 · Sign-off

    The approver's and BPO's turn. A clean draft: no review notes, no resolved comments, no placeholders. O2C: Maria Pabalan approves, Christopher Clarke is BPO. R2R: Ken Lee approves.

    Moves on when the approval is recorded on the card.

  6. 6 · Ready to publish

    Your turn. You make the PDF (Claude delivers the Word file only, D-064), remove the CLAUDE- prefix (D-056) and publish to the Training Hub.

    Moves on when the Hub link is on the card and the ticket is closed.

  7. 7 · Published

    On the Training Hub. A later change starts a new cycle from Drafting, and the published version stays live until the new one passes its gates.

Questions on each card

Each card's questions link opens that ticket's questions in a popup: copy them into the ADO ticket, edit them, mark them sent, and paste the SME's answers back. Claude applies the answers at the next session (D-069).

  • Group by theme and by who answers. Approvals, roles, navigation, configuration values, scope: one question per theme for each person.
  • Lettered parts keep a big question answerable. Each part lists the register items it closes. The SME answers one question; each item still closes on its own.
  • Across guides too. When the same gap sits in several guides, one question covers all of them, in one ticket, and the other cards point to it.
  • Split only when different people answer, or the items have nothing in common.
  • An answer that does not close the gap leaves the item open for the next round. An explicit scope decision can close an item. Nothing a source already confirms is asked again.

Comments in the Word guide

One review comment per item, at the place it matters most, and only for open items that still need an answer (D-067). Answered items, items carried over from topic drafts and non-blocking items live in the validation register and on this board. Every ACTION: REVIEW REQUIREMENT note keeps its comment (D-060), and every note and comment is gone before publication.

Where things live

  • The repository holds the documents: Word guides, Markdown previews, validation registers, decision logs and the numbering register. The validation register is the record of every question and answer.
  • This board holds the status: lane, people, links, next action, questions, weekly sync notes. Every change Claude commits to a guide updates its card in the same turn (D-068).
  • Changes made here are logged with who and when. At the start of each session Claude reads what changed since its last sync, applies recorded answers and meeting notes to the repository, and updates the sync time in the header.
  • Numbering is read-only here. The register in the repository is the authority; ask in chat to allocate or change a code.

File names

  • Working Word guide: CLAUDE-NNN-Guide Title PG vX.Y.docx. Published: NNN-Guide Title PG.pdf, without the prefix (D-056).
  • A document that is not a procedure guide takes the next number in the Other series: CLAUDE-O-0001-Title PG v0.1.docx (D-076).
