## Part 1: What do I need to know? (AR Collections Collection Activities)

### High-level process

##### Part Description

This guide explains how a Collections Officer records an email or telephone contact with a customer as a collection activity on the customer account. It covers creating the activity, selecting its type, entering a concise summary and notes, saving it and checking that it is visible. Reviewing previous activities is covered by the AR Collections Notes and Activities guide.

##### Initiation

- A Collections Officer has contacted a customer, or been contacted by them, about their account. `[VG-16]`

##### Stream

| Stream | Process Area | Key Roles |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts Receivable `[VG-17]` | AR Collections | • Collections Officer |

##### Process

| # | Action | Who | When | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Record a Customer Collection Activity](#1-record-a-customer-collection-activity) | Collections Officer | After each email or telephone contact with a customer | D365 |

### Roles and responsibilities

##### Overview

The Collections Officer records collection activities in their own D365 session.

| Tasks / Business Roles | Collections Officer |
|---|---|
| [Record a Customer Collection Activity](#1-record-a-customer-collection-activity) | R |

### Key concepts

##### Overview

Before you start, you need to know what a collection activity is, which activity types apply and where activities are recorded.

##### Collection activities

| Term | Description |
|---|---|
| **Collection activity** | A record on the customer account of a collection contact with the customer, such as an email or telephone call. It holds a summary and notes of the interaction. |
| **Activity type** | The kind of contact the activity records, for example email or phone. Use the approved activity types. `[VG-05, VG-06]` |
| **Summary and notes** | A concise summary of the interaction, with notes that give the details another officer needs. `[VG-09, VG-10]` |

##### Recording collection history

| Rule | Description |
|---|---|
| **Record at customer level** | Record collection activities against the customer account rather than against individual invoices, so the full history is visible in the account overview. `[VG-14]` |

## Part 2: How do I do it? (AR Collections Collection Activities)

### 1. Record a Customer Collection Activity

##### Description

Use this procedure to record an email or telephone contact as a collection activity on the customer account.

##### Responsibility

- Collections Officer: records each collection contact at customer level.

##### Initiation

- A Collections Officer has contacted a customer, or been contacted by them, about their account.

##### Procedure

Complete these steps to record a collection activity:

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| *PROCESS START* (light blue) | *Pre-requisites:*<br>• Access to D365 and to the **Credit and collections** module.<br>*Responsibility:*<br>• Collections Officer |
| 1. *Check* the **Entity**. | Confirm you are signed into the correct **Legal Entity** (top-right). |
| 2. *Click* **Modules**. | From the left-hand **Navigation Pane**, click **Modules**. |
| 3. *Navigate* to the **All customers** page. `[VG-01, VG-03]` | From the **Navigation Pane**, *select*:<br>• **Credit and collections >**<br>• **All customers** |
| 4. *Open* the customer account. `[VG-02]` | Find the customer and open the account. |
| 5. *Click* **New activity**. `[VG-04]` | Create a new activity for the customer. |
| 6. *Select* the activity type. `[VG-05]` | Select the type of contact, for example email or phone. |
| 7. *Enter* a summary. `[VG-07]` | Enter a concise summary of the interaction. |
| 8. *Enter* the notes. `[VG-08, VG-09]` | Enter the details of the contact. |
| 9. *Save* the activity. `[VG-12]` | Save the activity. |
| 10. *Check* the activity is listed. `[VG-13]` | Check that the activity appears in the customer's activities. |
| *PROCESS COMPLETE* (light green) | *Outcomes:*<br>• The collection activity is recorded on the customer account and is visible in the customer's activities. |
