## Part 1: What do I need to know? (AR Collections Workspace)

### High-level process

##### Part Description

This guide explains how a Collections Officer opens the **Customer credit and collections** workspace and finds the sections used in normal collection work: **Customers overdue**, **Payment journals not posted**, **Notes** and **Open payment journals**. The work done in each section is covered by its own guide. Interpreting the workspace indicators is outside the scope of this guide.

##### Initiation

- A Collections Officer starts their collection work for the day. `[VG-14]`

| Stream | Process Area | Key Roles |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts Receivable `[VG-15]` | AR Collections | • Collections Officer |

### Key concepts

##### Overview

Before you start, you need to know what the workspace is, which of its sections are used in AR Collections, and how it differs from the Credit and collections module.

##### The Customer credit and collections workspace

| Term | Description |
|---|---|
| **Workspace** | A D365 page that brings together the information and tasks for one area of work. The **Customer credit and collections** workspace is the starting point for collection work. `[VG-09]` |
| **Sections used in AR Collections** | **Customers overdue**, **Payment journals not posted**, **Notes** and **Open payment journals**. `[VG-04 to VG-07]` |
| **Other sections** | The workspace also shows sections such as **Open cases**, **Customers over credit limit** and **Collections letters not printed**. Which of these are used in AR Collections is pending confirmation. `[VG-08]` |

##### Workspaces and modules

| Term | Description |
|---|---|
| **Workspace or module** | The **Customer credit and collections** workspace opens from **Workspaces**. The **Credit and collections** module opens from **Modules** and lists pages such as **All customers** and **Aged balances**. `[VG-11]` |

## Part 2: How do I do it? (AR Collections Workspace)

### 1. Open the Customer Credit and Collections Workspace

##### Description

Use this procedure to open the **Customer credit and collections** workspace from **Workspaces** in the **Navigation Pane**.

##### Responsibility

- Collections Officer: opens the workspace to start collection work.

##### Initiation

- A Collections Officer starts their collection work for the day.

##### Procedure

Complete these steps to open the workspace:

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| *PROCESS START* (light blue) | *Pre-requisites:*<br>• Access to D365 and to the **Customer credit and collections** workspace.<br>*Responsibility:*<br>• Collections Officer |
| 1. *Check* the **Entity**. | Confirm you are signed into the correct **Legal Entity** (top-right). |
| 2. *Click* **Workspaces**. `[VG-01]` | From the left-hand **Navigation Pane**, click **Workspaces**. |
| 3. *Select* **Customer credit and collections**. `[VG-02]` | Select the **Customer credit and collections** workspace from the list. |
| 4. *Confirm* the workspace is open. `[VG-03, VG-10]` | Check that the **Customer credit and collections** workspace is displayed for the legal entity you selected in step 1. |
| *PROCESS COMPLETE* (light green) | *Outcomes:*<br>• The **Customer credit and collections** workspace is open. |

### 2. Locate Collection Work in the Workspace

##### Description

Use this procedure to find the workspace sections used in normal collection work. Each section links to the work covered by its own guide.

##### Responsibility

- Collections Officer: finds the workspace section needed for the task.

##### Initiation

- A Collections Officer needs to find where a collection task starts in the workspace. `[VG-14]`

##### Procedure

Complete these steps to locate collection work in the workspace:

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| *PROCESS START* (light blue) | *Pre-requisites:*<br>• The **Customer credit and collections** workspace is open (see [Open the Customer Credit and Collections Workspace](#1-open-the-customer-credit-and-collections-workspace)).<br>*Responsibility:*<br>• Collections Officer |
| 1. *Locate* **Customers overdue**. `[VG-04]` | Find **Customers overdue**. Use it to review customers with overdue balances. |
| 2. *Locate* **Payment journals not posted**. `[VG-05]` | Find **Payment journals not posted**. Use it to identify customer payment journals that have not been posted. |
| 3. *Locate* **Notes**. `[VG-06]` | Find **Notes**. Use it to review customer collection notes (see the AR Collections Notes and Activities guide). |
| 4. *Locate* **Open payment journals**. `[VG-07]` | Find **Open payment journals**. Use it to start investigating unallocated customer payments. |
| 5. *Open* the section you need. | Open the section for your task and continue with the guide for that task. |
| *PROCESS COMPLETE* (light green) | *Outcomes:*<br>• The workspace section for the task is open. |
