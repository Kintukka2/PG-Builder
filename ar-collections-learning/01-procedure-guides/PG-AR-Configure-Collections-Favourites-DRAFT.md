## 3. Purpose

Use this procedure to add the four D365 pages identified for AR Collections users to your Favourites.

Once added, you can open these pages directly from **Favourites** in the D365 navigation pane.

## 4. Audience

New AR Collections users setting up D365 before starting routine AR Collections work.

This is a day-one (P1) task. It needs no prior AR Collections process knowledge.

## 5. Scope

**In scope**

- Adding four pages to Favourites from the **Credit and collections** and **Accounts receivable** modules.
- Checking that all four pages appear under **Favourites**.

**Out of scope**

| Topic | Where it is covered |
|---|---|
| Switching legal entity | *Switch between legal entities in D365* (planned PG) |
| Creating, saving or pinning views on these pages | *Create and save a personalised D365 view* (planned PG) |
| Using the pages for collections work | Later PGs (see [Related learning content](#13-related-learning-content)) |
| Removing a favourite | Not yet documented (`VG-14`) |
| Adding other pages or workspaces to Favourites | Not covered by the source material |

## 6. Prerequisites

Before starting, confirm that:

- You can sign in to D365 (`VG-26`).
- You are working in the legal entity you use for AR Collections activities. To change it, see *Switch between legal entities in D365* (planned PG).
  - *Whether Favourites apply across legal entities is not yet confirmed (`VG-10`).*
- You can open the **Credit and collections** and **Accounts receivable** modules.
- The D365 navigation pane is visible (`VG-12`).

> **Note:** Which modules and pages you can see may depend on your D365 security access. If something listed in this guide is not available, see [Troubleshooting](#12-troubleshooting).

| Page | Navigation path |

| Aged balances | **Modules** > **Credit and collections** > **Aged balances** |

## 8. Pages to add

| # | Page | Source module | What the page is used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **All customers** | Credit and collections | Opening customer accounts and customer master details. |
| 2 | **Aged balances** (`VG-01`) | Credit and collections | Reviewing customer balances by ageing period. |
| 3 | **Customer payment journal** | Credit and collections | Identified in the training as a required favourite for AR Collections users. Its detailed purpose is not documented in the source material (`VG-18`). |
| 4 | **Open customer invoices** | Accounts receivable | Reviewing open customer invoices (`VG-19`). |

These four pages were identified in the *AR Training D365* session as the key pages to favourite.

When you have finished:

- You can open each of these pages from **Favourites**.

## 12. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Possible causes | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A module is not in the **Modules** list. | You may be signed in to a different D365 environment. Your security access may not include the module. This is a possible cause, not a confirmed diagnosis. | Check that you are in the correct D365 environment and legal entity. If the module is still missing, follow the access-support process: *[placeholder — process and owner TBC (`VG-16`)]*. |
| A page is not in the module menu. | You may have opened a different module. The page may be inside a menu group or submenu (`VG-02` to `VG-06`). The label may differ slightly from this guide (`VG-01`). Your security access may not include the page. This is a possible cause, not a confirmed diagnosis. | Check the module heading against the [Navigation path](#7-navigation-path). Do not use a page with a similar name unless you have confirmed it is the same page (`VG-29`). If the page is still missing, follow the access-support process (`VG-16`). |
| A page does not appear in **Favourites** after you selected its star. | The star selection may not have registered. | *Draft — action pending validation (`VG-07`).* Go back to the source module and find the page. Check the state of its favourite star before you select it again. |
| Favourites look different after changing legal entity or signing in again. | Not yet known (`VG-10`, `VG-11`). | Repeat the procedure for any missing page. Report what you saw to the PG owner *[placeholder]* so the guide can be updated. |
| A favourite is no longer needed. | — | Not yet documented (`VG-14`). |
