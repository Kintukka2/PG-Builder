## 1. Finding

Customer Pools is named in the Part 2 pack, but none of the pack's sources describe it in enough detail to draft anything. The Part 1 and Part 2 notes do not describe how Customer Pools are created, maintained or assigned, how they are used to filter work, or how they are used in day-to-day collections (source 4.0, "Customer Pools finding"). The readiness matrix rates it **Not ready** (source 4.3). The architecture and roadmap place it in Priority 4 and Release 4 (sources 4.1 and 4.2).

Nothing here describes what Customer Pools do in D365. That would be an assumption until the items below are answered.

## 2. Evidence needed before drafting

Customer Pools can move to the active queue only when it meets the definition of ready for drafting (source 2): a clear task boundary, a known starting path, demonstrated actions, exact labels, recorded gaps and an assigned module. That needs an SME playback or a new recording.
