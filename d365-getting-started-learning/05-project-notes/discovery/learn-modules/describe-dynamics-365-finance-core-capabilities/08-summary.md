# Summary

Completed

* 4 minutes

This module introduced Dynamics 365 Finance, a comprehensive solution for managing financial operations in multi-company, multi-currency environments. It emphasizes how Finance supports compliance, efficiency, and strategic decision-making.

Core features include a general ledger for centralized financial records, accounts receivable and payable for managing customer and vendor transactions, and cash and bank management for maintaining accounts and forecasting cash flow. Other modules cover budgeting, fixed assets, expense management, and cost accounting to support planning and compliance.

Advanced features such as asset leasing, subscription billing, and credit and collection management address specialized needs. Regulatory tools help ensure compliance with country/region-specific requirements. The system also supports multi-currency management, tax configuration, and electronic payment formats for efficient global operations.

Now that you reviewed this module, you should be able to:

* Describe Finance capabilities and features.
* Describe reporting currency and accounting currency.
* Describe tax capabilities.
* Describe cost accounting concepts.
* Describe electronic payment formats.
