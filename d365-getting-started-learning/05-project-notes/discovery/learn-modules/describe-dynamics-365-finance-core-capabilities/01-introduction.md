# Introduction

Completed

* 8 minutes

Dynamics 365 Finance offers powerful capabilities for managing financial operations across global organizations. This module explores the core features that enable businesses to handle complex financial processes, manage multiple currencies, ensure tax compliance, control costs, and process payments efficiently.

Imagine you're a financial controller at a multinational company with operations in several countries or regions. Your organization needs to manage transactions in multiple currencies while maintaining accurate financial reporting. You must ensure compliance with different tax regulations across regions, track and analyze costs effectively, and streamline payment processes to vendors. Additionally, you need to understand how reporting currency differs from accounting currency to consolidate financial statements across all legal entities.

This module addresses these challenges by exploring the key capabilities of Dynamics 365 Finance. You learn about the comprehensive features that support day-to-day financial operations, including general ledger, accounts payable and receivable, budgeting, and asset management.

You discover how Finance handles multi-currency scenarios through reporting currency and accounting currency concepts. These features enable organizations to conduct business in various currencies while maintaining a single currency for financial reporting.

The module covers tax capabilities that help organizations comply with regional requirements. You explore how sales tax codes, tax groups, and settlement periods work together to calculate and report taxes accurately.

You also learn about cost accounting concepts that provide insights into operational efficiency. Cost accounting helps managers track, analyze, and control costs to make informed business decisions.

Finally, you examine electronic payment formats that streamline vendor payments and reduce manual processing. These capabilities support various payment methods and formats required by different banking systems.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you're able to:

* Describe Finance capabilities, features, and use cases.
* Describe reporting currency and accounting currency.
* Describe Dynamics 365 Finance tax capabilities.
* Describe cost accounting concepts.
* Describe electronic payment formats.
