# Introduction

Completed

* 7 minutes

Finance and operations apps includes several modules, areas, and pages to help you perform various tasks to support business scenarios in many industries.

This learning module will help you find your way around the application, quickly locate the pages that you need to complete your tasks, and understand how to use workspaces.

## Navigation elements

The primary navigation concepts in finance and operations apps are:

* Dashboard
* Navigation pane
* Workspaces
* Tiles
* Navigation search

Watch this video to see the navigation elements in finance and operations apps.
