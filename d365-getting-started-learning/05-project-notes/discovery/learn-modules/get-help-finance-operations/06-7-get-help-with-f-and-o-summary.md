Several other resources are available to you.

* **Microsoft Dynamics Lifecycle Services** - Provides a cloud-based collaborative workspace that customers and partners can use to manage finance and operations apps projects, from presales to implementations and operations. This site is useful in all phases of an implementation. Go to [https://lcs.dynamics.com](https://lcs.dynamics.com/?azure-portal=true).
* **Support blog** - This blog provides tips and tricks that are posted by the finance and operations apps Support team. Go to [https://cloudblogs.microsoft.com/dynamics365](https://cloudblogs.microsoft.com/dynamics365/?azure-portal=true).
* **Dynamics community** - Here, you can find community-authored blogs, forums, and videos. Go to [https://community.dynamics.com/](https://community.dynamics.com/?azure-portal=true).
