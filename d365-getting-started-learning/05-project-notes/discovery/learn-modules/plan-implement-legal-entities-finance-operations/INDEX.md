# Plan and implement legal entities in finance and operations apps

Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/plan-implement-legal-entities-finance-operations/

Summary: A legal entity is an organization that is identified through registration with a legal authority. Legal entities can enter into contracts and are required to prepare statements that report on their performance. You must plan and configure your organization and create legal entities as one of the first steps of implementing finance and operations apps.
