# Create a legal entity

Completed

* 10 minutes

This module discusses how to create a legal entity and prepare it for financial management, which is the first phase of the implementation.

A *legal entity* is an organization that has a registered or legislated legal structure. Legal entities can enter into legal contracts and are required to prepare statements that report on their performance.

The country or region of a legal entity's primary address determines the available country-specific features in finance and operations apps. Some regions have regulatory requirements that enable or disable certain functionality, forms, fields, reports, and inquiries. Understanding localization is important before selecting a country or region.

A company is the only type of legal entity you can create, and it is identified by a fixed four-character **Company ID**. Because this **Company ID** can't be changed, it's important to carefully plan for a naming convention that is essential. Users can access data only for the company they are signed into and must have the appropriate security permissions based on their role.

A search name is an alternate name that can be used to search for this legal entity.

## Registration IDs

Different countries and regions have unique regulations for recording registration numbers or IDs. These numbers, such as Social Security Number (SSN), Tax Identification Number (TIN), and European VAT ID (EU VAT ID), are issued by state offices and used for tax, customs, and other official purposes.

Finance and operations apps provide a unified framework for managing registration numbers while accommodating country-specific requirements, particularly in Europe. The Registration category serves as the approved identifier for each country or region.

When a legal entity is created, the next step is configuring number sequences for the new company.
