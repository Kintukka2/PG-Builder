# Exercise - Configure a new legal entity

Completed

* 15 minutes

## Scenario

A legal entity is an organization that is identified through registration with a legal authority. Legal entities can enter into contracts and are required to prepare statements that report on their performance.

You will create a legal entity in finance and operations apps.

1. Go to **Organization administration** > **Organizations** > **Legal entities**.
2. Select **New**.
3. In the **Name** field, type a value.
4. In the **Company** field, type a value.
5. In the **Country/region** field, enter or select a value.
6. Select **OK**.
7. In the **General** section, provide the following general information about the legal entity: Enter a **search name** if it is required.
8. If this legal entity is being used as a **consolidation** company, set the **Use for financial consolidation process** slider to **Yes**.
9. If this legal entity is being used as an **elimination** company, set the **Use for financial elimination process** slider to **Yes**.
10. Expand the **Addresses** section.
11. Select **Edit** and enter address information, such as the street name and number, postal code, and city.
12. Select **OK**.
13. Expand the **Contact information** section.
14. To enter a new Contact information record, select **Add**. To modify an existing communication record, select **Advanced**.
15. Enter information about methods of communication, such as email addresses, URLs, and telephone numbers.
16. Expand the **Statutory reporting** section, and enter the registration numbers that are used for statutory reporting.
17. Expand the **Registration numbers** section, and enter any information required by the legal entity.
18. Expand the **Bank account information** section, and enter bank accounts and routing numbers for the legal entity.
19. Expand the **Foreign trade and logistics** section, and enter shipping information for the legal entity.
20. Expand the **Number sequences** section to view the number sequences that are associated with the legal entity.
21. Expand the **Dashboard Image** section to view or change the logo and/or dashboard image that is associated with the legal entity.
22. Expand the **Tax registration** section, and enter the registration numbers that are used to report to tax authorities.
23. Expand the **Tax 1099** section, and enter 1099 information for the legal entity. (This is only necessary for a US-based company.)
24. Select **Save**.
