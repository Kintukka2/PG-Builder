# Introduction

Completed

* 6 minutes

This module introduces the main financial management features in Dynamics 365 Finance. You learn about charts of accounts, financial dimensions, accounting structures, and periodic financial processes. Dynamics 365 Finance offers tools to help you manage accounting, financial reporting, and analysis. It supports the full process of classifying, recording, storing, and summarizing financial transactions for a company's assets, liabilities, and capital.

Throughout this module, you explore fiscal and ledger calendars, the chart of accounts, financial dimensions, and the accounting structure. You also learn about financial journals, reporting capabilities such as Financial reporting and Power BI, and the cash and bank management features used to process payments, receipts, and reconciliations. In addition, the module covers allocations and accruals, financial period close processes, and the fundamentals of financial consolidation.

Dynamics 365 Finance also includes emerging AI capabilities through Finance agents, which streamline tasks such as financial reconciliation and collections by providing intelligent insights directly within Microsoft 365 tools.

A business owner oversees multiple retail locations and relies on Dynamics 365 Finance to simplify the monthly financial closing process. The business owner uses the platform’s chart of accounts and financial dimensions to categorize and analyze transactions by store and department. When reconciling cash and bank accounts, the built-in Finance agents automatically identify discrepancies and highlight outstanding collections, enabling the business owner to resolve issues efficiently within the Microsoft 365 workspace. This seamless integration and intelligent automation save the business owner valuable time and ensure accurate financial reporting.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you're able to:

* Describe the concept of a fiscal calendar and a ledger calendar.
* Describe charts of accounts, including main accounts.
* Describe financial dimensions, dimension sets, and financial tags concepts.
* Describe the accounting structure.
* Describe financial journal concepts.
* Describe Financial reporting capabilities.
* Describe cash and bank management concepts.
* Describe use cases for allocations and accruals.
* Describe periodic financial processes.
