# Introduction

Completed

* 8 minutes

This module provides an overview of the core processes that support accounts payable, accounts receivable, and credit and collections in finance and operations apps. You learn how organizations configure payment methods, manage vendors and customers, and process invoices across purchasing and sales activities. The module also introduces posting profiles and explains how financial dimensions flow through journals and transactions.

In addition, this module covers settlement processes for both vendors and customers, including payment application, matching principles, and netting. You explore credit management and collection activities that help organizations maintain healthy credit practices and reduce outstanding balances. The module also introduces subscription billing concepts used for recurring invoicing scenarios.

Dynamics 365 Finance uses AI features like Copilot to provide summaries and insights about vendors, customers, orders, and collections, making it easier for users to review financial activity and prioritize tasks.

Consider the following example. A company is currently managing its daily financial operations using Dynamics 365 Finance. The accounts payable team configures payment methods and processes vendor invoices, while the accounts receivable staff manages customer records and issues sales invoices. As a new batch of purchase orders and sales invoices is processed, the finance manager uses posting profiles to ensure the correct financial dimensions are applied throughout all transactions. Using the system’s AI-powered Copilot summaries and insights, the team quickly reviews key information across vendors and customers, enabling them to identify priority payments and flag overdue accounts. At the end of the week, the credit and collections department uses the module’s credit management and collections features to monitor outstanding balances, apply payments, and initiate collection activities as needed. The integration of netting and settlement processes further streamlines financial operations, ensuring that both vendor and customer obligations are efficiently reconciled and reported.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you're able to:

* Describe payment setup.
* Describe core accounts payable components, including vendors, purchase orders, and vendor invoices.
* Describe posting profiles and how they work with account (and offset account) dimensions in journals.
* Describe vendor payments and settlements, including three-way matching concepts.
* Describe core accounts receivable components, including customers, customer invoices, and free text invoices.
* Describe customer/vendor netting.
* Describe credit management processes.
* Describe the collection process.
* Describe the concept of subscription billing.
