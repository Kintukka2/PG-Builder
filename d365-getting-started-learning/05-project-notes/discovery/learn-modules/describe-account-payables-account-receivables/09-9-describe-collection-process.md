# Describe the collections process in Dynamics 365 Finance

Completed

* 15 minutes

The **Collections** page provides a centralized view where accounts receivable collections information is managed. Collections managers can use this centralized view to manage collections. Collections agents can begin the collections process either from customer lists that are generated by using predefined collection criteria or from the **Customers** page.

Before you start to set up or work with collections, you should understand the following concepts:

* Customer aging snapshots contain aged balance information at a specific point in time.
* Collections customer pools help you organize your work.
* Collections agents can have their own customer pools.
* List pages organize collections customers, activities, and cases.
* All collections information for a customer is on one page, and you can take action from that page.
* Interest and fees can be waived, reinstated, or reversed in one step.
* Write-off transactions can be created in one step.
* Nonsufficient funds (NSF) payments can be processed in one step.

## Aging report

The Customer aging report displays the balances that are due from customers, sorted by date interval or aging period. To display the aging report, first you need to configure the aging period definitions.

[![Screenshot of Aging period definitions.](media/09-6-9-aging.png)](media/6-9-aging.png#lightbox)

Each aging period definition that is configured corresponds to a column in the aging balance page when the aging analysis is performed.

You can run the **Age customer balances process** under the **Periodic tasks** menu in the **Credit and collections** module for a given date, along with the aging period definition. The date, together with the unit and interval information that you enter on the **Periods** FastTab in the preceding page, is used to calculate a date interval for each aging period in the aging balance page under the **Collections** menu.

You can view this report in reporting currency by changing **Currency for aging**.

[![Screenshot of aged balances with the Currency for aging selection highlighted.](media/09-6-9-aged-balances.png)](media/6-9-aged-balances.png#lightbox)

## Collection letter

The first step to process collection letters is to configure the Collection letter sequence.

Collection letter sequences can be created based on the terms of payment. On the **Overview** FastTab, you can create a sequence of collection letters with the series of notifications along with the fee involved and posting account. On the **Note** FastTab, you can write the note you want to mention in the collection letter.

The next step is to run the **Creation of collection letter** under the **Collection letter** menu.

Based on the rules previously defined, collection letters are created, which are available on the **Review and process collection letters** page under the **Collection letter** menu.

You can post or cancel the collection letter after review and then send the letter if necessary.

[![Screenshot of the Review and process collection letters screen.](media/09-6-9-collection-letters.png)](media/6-9-collection-letters.png#lightbox)

## Workspaces for credit and collections

Dynamics 365 Finance provides two workspaces that help credit and collections teams manage overdue receivables, monitor customer risk, and streamline collection activities: **Credit and collections** and **Collections coordinator**. These workspaces centralize aging data, collection tasks, cases, and follow-up activities and include embedded Power BI analytics that highlight trends and issues that require attention.

### Credit and collections workspace

The **Credit and collections** workspace provides a consolidated view of customer aging, overdue invoices, credit limits, and collection cases. Users can quickly identify customers who are over their credit limit, invoices that require follow-up, and accounts with active dispute or collection activities. Embedded analytics show key metrics such as aging buckets, top overdue customers, and payment trends to help teams prioritize collection efforts and reduce outstanding balances.

[![Screenshot of the Credit and collections workspace.](media/09-6-9-workspace.png)](media/6-9-workspace.png#lightbox)

### Collections coordinator workspace

The **Collections coordinator** workspace focuses on day-to-day collections activity. It presents a queue of collection cases, notes, tasks, and follow-up items for each customer. Users can track promises to pay, log collection attempts, send statements, review disputes, and manage the full collection process from one place. Power BI visuals help coordinators spot bottlenecks, monitor productivity, and prioritize accounts that need immediate action.

The **Collections coordinator** workspace includes an optional **Summary by Copilot** panel that provides AI generated insights about a customer's outstanding balances, aging trends, promises to pay, and recent collection activity. This summary helps collections agents quickly identify accounts that require follow up and prioritize the next action without reviewing multiple pages.
