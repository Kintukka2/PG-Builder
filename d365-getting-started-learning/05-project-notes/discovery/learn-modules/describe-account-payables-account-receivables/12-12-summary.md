# Summary

Completed

* 4 minutes

This module reviewed the key accounting processes that support accounts payable, accounts receivable, and credit and collections in Dynamics 365 Finance. You explored how organizations configure payment methods, manage vendors and customers, and process invoices across purchasing and sales activities. The module also explained how posting profiles and financial dimensions influence account selection during journal entry.

You learned how settlements work for both vendor and customer transactions, including matching principles and netting. The module also covered credit management and collection activities that help organizations monitor risk and manage overdue balances. Finally, the module introduced subscription billing concepts used for recurring invoicing.

AI and Copilot features support these financial processes by offering summaries and insights across vendors, customers, purchase orders, and collections to help users work more efficiently.

Now that you reviewed this module, you should be able to:

* Describe payment setup.
* Describe core accounts payable components, including vendors, purchase orders, and vendor invoices.
* Describe posting profiles and how they work with account (and offset account) dimensions in journals.
* Describe vendor payments and settlements, including three-way matching concepts.
* Describe core accounts receivable components, including customers, customer invoices, and free text invoices.
* Describe customer/vendor netting.
* Describe credit management processes.
* Describe the collection process.
* Describe the concept of subscription billing.
