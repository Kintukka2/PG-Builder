# Configure reports

Completed

* 1 minute

Dynamics 365 is designed so that you have many useful reports provided out of
the box. You can easily make many modifications to them to meet your business
needs.

Select this link to learn more about the financial reports available:
[Exercise - Generate and explore a financial report](/en-us/training/modules/work-analytics-reporting-finance-operations/6-exercise-1)

The concept of Business Document Management enables us to edit business document
templates by using Microsoft 365. Many changes can be made to existing
reports **without code.**

You may read more about configurable business documents here: [Business document management overview](/en-us/dynamics365/fin-ops-core/dev-itpro/analytics/er-business-document-management)

Many times, a company has a business need to add fields, or change their
positioning, in standard reports, such as a check or an invoice. Microsoft makes
it easy to do by creating templates. This is thanks to the Electronic Reporting
(ER) tool and integration with Microsoft Excel and Word. Editing templates
requires a configured SharePoint integration to host the files while you work on them.

Further information may be found here: [Add new fields to a business document template in Microsoft Excel](/en-us/dynamics365/fin-ops-core/dev-itpro/analytics/er-bdm-add-field-to-excel-template?toc=/dynamics365/commerce/toc.json#configure-business-document-management-to-edit-templates).
