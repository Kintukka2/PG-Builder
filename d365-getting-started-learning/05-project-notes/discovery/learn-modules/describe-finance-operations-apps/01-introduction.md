# Introduction

Completed

* 8 minutes

This module provides an overview of the capabilities and business value of finance and operations apps. These apps include Dynamics 365 Finance, Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management, Dynamics 365 Commerce, Dynamics 365 Human Resources, and Dynamics 365 Project Operations. These apps help businesses manage their global financial systems, operational business processes, and supply chains, empowering people to make fast, informed decisions.

Imagine you're a business owner in the retail industry. You have multiple stores and are struggling to manage your finances, supply chain, and human resources effectively. You're looking for a solution that can simplify these processes, provide real-time insights, and integrate with other tools you're already using. This situation is where Microsoft's suite of finance and operations apps comes into play.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you're able to:

* Describe enterprise resource planning (ERP).
* Describe use cases for using AI tools in finance and operations apps, including Copilot and agent capabilities.
* Navigate efficiently within and across finance and operations apps.

Learn about the capabilities and business value of Microsoft's finance and operations apps. After completing this module, you can describe enterprise resource planning (ERP). You can also describe how the finance and operations apps, Copilot, and agents can be used to improve your business processes. Finally, you can navigate the finance and operations user interface.
