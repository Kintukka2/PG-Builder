# Exercise - Explore Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management

Completed

* 15 minutes

## Scenario

Now that you've learned about the basics of Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management, and the modules and features that it includes, it’s your turn to explore.

1. After you've logged in to the lab environment, by following the instructions above, you can select tiles on the **Home** page to open workspaces. For example, **Purchase order preparation**, or **Released product maintenance**.

   ![Screenshot of the Home page with the Purchase order preparation and Released product maintenance tile s highlighted.](media/17-workspaces-scm-ss.png)

   You close a workspace by selecting the **X** in the top right.
2. Select the **Modules** icon in the navigation pane on the left, and open a module, and explore the many pages in that module. For example, **Inventory management**.

   ![Screenshot of the navigation pane, with the Inventory management module selected.](media/17-modules-icon-scm-ss.png)

   Note

   In this lab environment, there are more Dynamics 365 apps installed than Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management. Therefore, you will also see modules that are not part of Supply Chain Management.
3. When have finished exploring Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management, you can close the lab environment.
