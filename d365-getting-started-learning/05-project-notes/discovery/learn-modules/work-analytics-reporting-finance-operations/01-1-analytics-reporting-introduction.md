# Introduction

Completed

* 2 minutes

A report in finance and operations apps can be defined simply as a structured presentation of data.
Under ideal circumstances, reports materialize data in such a way that it allows the user to make informed decisions.
Finance and operations apps supports a broad spectrum of information access scenarios that include, but are not limited to, the following:

* Cross company all-up financial reporting
* Analytical dashboards and tiles
* Electronically-transferable funds
* Customer sales invoices
* Checks and tax documents

In this module, you will learn about:

* Different types of reports and inquiries in finance and operations apps
* Financial reporting
* Configuring finance and operations apps for Microsoft Power Platform
